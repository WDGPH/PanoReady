# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the PanoReady community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience; and
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission; and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behaviour. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately through the contact options on the [Wellington-Dufferin-Guelph Public Health website](https://wdgpublichealth.ca/contact-us/). Do not include student records or other sensitive project data.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly as maintainer availability permits. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will use these community-impact guidelines when determining consequences:

1. **Correction** — A private warning with an explanation of the violation and an opportunity to correct the behaviour.
2. **Warning** — A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with the people involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — Permanent removal from community interaction for a pattern of violations or serious misconduct.
